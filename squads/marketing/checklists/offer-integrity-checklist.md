# Offer Integrity Checklist

**Checklist ID:** MKT-CL-006
**Referenced by:** `@marketing:offer-lead` (Bounty) — `*offer-build`, `*integrity-screen`,
`*guarantee-design`; consumed by `@marketing:funnel-lead` before a funnel carries the offer
**Applies to:** any offer about to be published, any guarantee about to be promised, any ladder
rung about to be sold
**Purpose:** apply two tests at once — is the offer **constructed** (does it address the term
that is actually binding), and is it **honest** (is every urgency, scarcity, proof and guarantee
claim true and checkable).

**Method source, and what kind of source it is.** Alex Hormozi, *$100M Offers* (2021), with
lead-flow mechanics from *$100M Leads* (2023), named separately. These are **practitioner
manuals with a track record** — method distilled by an operator from their own operating
history. They supply structure and sequence, and no research programme currently does. They do
not supply a sampled population, a control condition or dispersion, so no figure in them is an
estimate of what will happen here.

This differs in kind from the sources behind `@marketing:brand-lead` (Sharp, reporting
Ehrenberg-Bass) and `@marketing:demand-lead` (Binet and Field, analysing the IPA Databank),
which are **research programmes with published data**. Both are legitimate bases for a
recommendation. Section G exists because collapsing the two makes an offer document unauditable.

## A. Gate — the inputs this offer is built on

*Any FAIL here blocks approval. An offer constructed on unsettled inputs is rebuilt when they land.*

- [ ] **A.1** The **price** is settled and consumed from `@products:pricing-strategist`, with
  the artifact named. The offer is built **on** the price.
- [ ] **A.2** The document contains **no** price point set here, no discount authorised here and
  no packaging tier invented here. *(A finding that the price is wrong is routed back, not acted
  on in this document.)*
- [ ] **A.3** The **position** — market category, competitive alternatives, segment — is consumed
  from `@products:positioning-lead`, not invented.
- [ ] **A.4** The buyer named has the problem, the money and the authority to act, with the
  evidence class stated. *(If not, this is not an offer problem — route it.)*
- [ ] **A.5** Every element of the core deliverable is deliverable **today**, at volume, confirmed
  by someone who owns delivery.

## B. Construction — the value equation

*[CLASS: PRACTITIONER HEURISTIC — Hormozi, $100M Offers, 2021]*

- [ ] **B.1** All four terms are stated: dream outcome, perceived likelihood of achievement, time
  delay, effort and sacrifice.
- [ ] **B.2** The dream outcome is written in the buyer's words, not the company's, and describes
  what the product produces **today**.
- [ ] **B.3** Perceived likelihood is addressed with real, attributable evidence — not with a
  larger promise. *(Inflating the outcome raises the claim and lowers the belief at the same time.)*
- [ ] **B.4** **Both denominator terms were worked, not just described.** Time to first outcome and
  buyer effort each have a named reduction and an owner. *(This is where offers are usually lost
  and rarely worked, because raising the promise is a writing task and lowering time and effort is
  an operations task.)*
- [ ] **B.5** Effort is counted beyond money — what the buyer must learn, migrate, abandon or risk.
- [ ] **B.6** Exactly one **binding term** is named, with the evidence that selects it.
- [ ] **B.7** No weight, coefficient or formula is assigned to the value equation terms. *(The
  source states a relationship, not a weighted formula. Inventing weights is invention.)*

## C. The offer itself

- [ ] **C.1** Obstacles were enumerated **first** and exhaustively, then a solution attached to
  each. *(Built in reverse, the stack is a feature list in a benefit costume.)*
- [ ] **C.2** Every obstacle with no solution attached is listed, and the document says where it
  is disclosed to the buyer.
- [ ] **C.3** Each bonus answers one **named** objection. Bonuses with no objection attached are
  removed as padding.
- [ ] **C.4** Delivery vehicles are stated, and any vehicle change that moves cost to serve is
  routed to `@products:pricing-strategist`.
- [ ] **C.5** The offer name states who it is for and what changes.
- [ ] **C.6** Each ladder rung delivers a **complete** outcome for its own narrower promise.
  *(A rung that only makes sense as a step to the next is bait.)*

## D. Guarantee

- [ ] **D.1** The guarantee type is named from the taxonomy, and the trigger condition is stated
  in terms the buyer can **observe**.
- [ ] **D.2** The window is real date arithmetic and **spans the time to first outcome** from B.4.
  *(A window that closes before the outcome could arrive is not a guarantee.)*
- [ ] **D.3** The remedy is specific: what is returned, by when, by whom.
- [ ] **D.4** The claim process is written out and is **no harder than the purchase process**.
- [ ] **D.5** Every condition is one a good-faith buyer can meet **and can verify they met**.
- [ ] **D.6** Redemption exposure has been agreed by a named human with the authority to honour it.
- [ ] **D.7** Where no honest guarantee is possible, the document says so and states plainly what
  the buyer is risking. *(A weaker offer that is intact beats a stronger one that is not.)*
- [ ] **D.8** No redemption rate, conversion lift or "which type performs best" claim appears
  unless read from the publication and labelled PRACTITIONER HEURISTIC.

## E. Evidence and proof

- [ ] **E.1** Every testimonial, case study, logo and metric is real and attributable to a named,
  real party.
- [ ] **E.2** No composite of several clients is presented as one.
- [ ] **E.3** Any outcome figure quoted has a source that can be named on request.
- [ ] **E.4** Roadmap is disclosed **as roadmap**, with no implied date not committed internally.
- [ ] **E.5** Where the evidence does not exist yet, the claim is lowered to what can be shown
  rather than filled with something plausible.

## F. Integrity screen — **BLOCKING**

*PASS / **BLOCK** / N/A. A single BLOCK stops the offer. Not scored, not weighted, not averaged.*

- [ ] **F.1 Urgency.** Every deadline is a real date with a real consequence, correctly attributed
  to whoever owns it. No "ends tonight" that reopens, no timer that resets per visitor, no
  deadline created for effect.
  > **BLOCK example:** "Enrolment closes at midnight" on a page that reopens tomorrow.
  > **Compliant form:** state the real cohort start date, or run with no deadline and say plainly
  > what does and does not change if they wait.

- [ ] **F.2 Scarcity.** No seat count, cohort cap, slot or allocation is claimed unless it is
  tracked somewhere checkable and would actually be enforced.
  > **Compliant form:** publish a real limit and enforce it, or remove the claim entirely.

- [ ] **F.3 Guarantee honesty.** No conditions engineered to be unmeetable, no window shorter than
  the first outcome, no claim process harder than the purchase, no guarantee operations has not
  agreed to honour.

- [ ] **F.4 Proof.** No invented testimonial, no composite presented as one client, no earnings
  claim without a source, no borrowed logo, no fabricated review.

- [ ] **F.5 Anchoring.** No crossed-out price that was never charged. No stack "value total"
  assembled from prices nobody paid.
  > **Compliant form:** anchor against a real alternative cost the buyer already carries — current
  > spend, or the cost of the problem continuing.

- [ ] **F.6 Continuity.** Auto-renewal, uplift and the cancellation path are disclosed **before**
  payment. Cancellation is at most as hard as buying.

- [ ] **F.7 Completeness.** No component necessary to the promised outcome is withheld to force an
  upgrade. Each rung's promise is reachable at that rung.

- [ ] **F.8 Consent.** Lead capture is explicit opt-in with stated purpose and stated frequency,
  and unsubscribe works immediately. No purchased or scraped lists, no pre-checked boxes.

- [ ] **F.9 Outcome claim.** No outcome is described that the product does not produce today.

### The three whole-offer tests

- [ ] **F.10 Reversal.** If the buyer learned exactly how this offer was constructed — every
  deadline's real basis, every stated value's real basis, every condition's real purpose — would
  they still consider it fair dealing?
- [ ] **F.11 Delivery.** If every buyer accepted every element on the day they bought, could
  operations honour it? *(An offer operations cannot deliver is a refund queue with a landing page.)*
- [ ] **F.12 Durability.** Would the buyer defend this purchase internally six months from now,
  knowing what we know today? *(A purchase they cannot defend is a churn event already scheduled.)*

## G. Evidence class and attribution integrity

- [ ] **G.1** Every claim carries an evidence class: `PRACTITIONER HEURISTIC`, `EMPIRICAL`,
  `OUR DATA` or `UNVERIFIED`.
- [ ] **G.2** **No practitioner heuristic is stated in the voice of an empirical finding.** The
  document says which claims come from a manual distilled from one operator's experience and
  which come from a research programme with a sampled population. *(This is a **critical** item:
  a laundered heuristic passes review by looking measured, then gets quoted in a budget case.)*
- [ ] **G.3** Concepts from *$100M Leads* (2023) are attributed to that work and not folded into
  *$100M Offers* (2021).
- [ ] **G.4** No price multiple, conversion lift, guarantee redemption rate, list-size threshold
  or revenue figure is quoted from either book **from memory**. Either it was read from the
  publication, or the mechanism is described without the number.
- [ ] **G.5** A figure read from the publication is still labelled PRACTITIONER HEURISTIC — it
  describes the author's businesses, not a measured population, and it carries no dispersion.
- [ ] **G.6** No practitioner heuristic is used to justify a **forecast number** or a target. It
  may justify construction and sequencing only.
- [ ] **G.7** No UNVERIFIED figure is load-bearing for a decision.
- [ ] **G.8** The document does not claim that an offer or conversion improvement constitutes
  brand growth. *(Different mechanism, different population, different measurement window — that
  belongs to `@marketing:brand-lead` and `@marketing:demand-lead`.)*

## H. Boundary and capture

- [ ] **H.1** No price, discount or packaging decision appears (`@products:pricing-strategist`).
- [ ] **H.2** No market category, competitive alternative or segment is defined here
  (`@products:positioning-lead`).
- [ ] **H.3** No media budget, source selection or brand-vs-activation split appears
  (`@marketing:demand-lead`).
- [ ] **H.4** No instrument is designed here; constructs are handed to `@marketing:analytics-lead`,
  and no lift is claimed without a test that agent designed.
- [ ] **H.5** No implementation (`@dev`), no test plan (`@qa`), no release step (`@devops`), no
  story (`@sm`). Product change implied by a time-delay or effort fix routes to `@pm`.
- [ ] **H.6** Written to the repository with an owner and a review date, not left in a transcript.
- [ ] **H.7** At least one falsifying observation is named: what would tell us this offer failed,
  and by when.

## Verdict

**Screen:** any **BLOCK** in Section F → `BLOCKED`, regardless of every score below.
**Gate:** any FAIL in Section A → `BLOCKED`.
**Score:** checked items ÷ total applicable items across B–E and G–H.

| Verdict | Condition |
|---|---|
| `APPROVED` | Section F clear, Section A clean, score ≥ 90%, no FAIL in B.4, D.4, G.2 or G.4 |
| `CONCERNS` | Section F clear, Section A clean, score 75–89%, or a single FAIL in a non-critical item |
| `REWORK` | Score below 75%, or any FAIL in B.4 (denominator not worked), D.4 (claim harder than purchase), G.2 (heuristic stated as finding) or G.4 (figure recalled from memory) |
| `BLOCKED` | Any **BLOCK** in Section F, or any FAIL in Section A |

**Critical items regardless of score:** A.1–A.5, B.4, D.2, D.4, G.2, G.4.

**If proceeding against the screen:** named decider, dated. Logged as decided against advice.

## Priority fix order

1. **Section F** — integrity. A blocked element is replaced before anything else is discussed;
   there is no version of this work where an effective false claim is a trade-off.
2. **Section A** — inputs. An offer built on an unsettled price is rebuilt when the price lands.
3. **G.2 / G.4** — evidence class and recalled figures. An attribution defect makes the whole
   document unauditable and is invisible once it propagates.
4. **B.4 / D** — the denominator terms and the guarantee. These are the two levers most often
   left untouched, and between them they usually contain the binding constraint.
5. **C / E** — stack completeness and proof.
6. **H** — boundary and capture.
