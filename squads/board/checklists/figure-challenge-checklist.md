# Figure Challenge and Assurance Checklist

**Checklist ID:** BRD-CL-004
**Referenced by:** audit-lead
**Applied to:** any figure challenge, judgements register, control review, assurance map, fraud-risk
assessment, auditor-independence assessment or private-session record before it reaches the board
**Purpose:** Enforce the three states a control is described in (**described / operated / tested**),
the assertion chain behind every figure (**what, who, on what basis, verified by whom**), and the
absolute prohibition on this squad issuing anything that reads as an audit opinion.

## 1. The assertion — restated before it is examined

- [ ] **BLOCKING.** The assertion is restated **precisely**, not summarized. Not "revenue grew 30%" but "management asserts that recognized revenue for the period was X, on basis Y, compared with a prior period computed on basis Z"
- [ ] **BLOCKING.** The **assertor is named** — a person or a function. "The system says" is not an assertion; a system is configured by someone, and that person is the assertor
- [ ] The **basis** is classified: measured / computed / estimated / judged
- [ ] Where computed, the source data is named, and it is stated whether that source has been reconciled to anything independent

## 2. Definition stability — the commonest distortion, and usually not deliberate

- [ ] **BLOCKING.** For any period comparison, the artifact confirms whether the **definition changed inside the period compared**
- [ ] A changed definition presented as a movement is described as a definition change, not as a movement
- [ ] Where the definition changed, the artifact states the figure on **both** definitions, or states that it cannot be restated and that the comparison is therefore invalid
- [ ] The definition-stability check is marked `CONSTRUCTION`

## 3. Judgements and their direction

- [ ] Every point where a person chose between defensible alternatives is identified: recognition timing, cut-off, classification, allocation, capitalization versus expense, provision and reserve levels, segment boundaries, cohort definitions, metric denominators
- [ ] For each: the choice made, the **alternative defensible choice**, and who made it
- [ ] **BLOCKING.** The **range** is stated — what the headline figure would be at the conservative end of each judgement and at the aggressive end. Not the point estimate alone
- [ ] **BLOCKING.** The **direction test** was applied: are the judgements independently distributed, or do they all lean the same way? Where individually defensible choices all favour the same conclusion, **the pattern is the finding** — every choice may be defensible and the pattern still means something
- [ ] Consistency with the prior period is checked: were these the same choices, and if any changed, why now?
- [ ] The direction test is marked `CONSTRUCTION`

## 4. Falsifier and independent test

- [ ] **BLOCKING.** The artifact names what observation would show the figure to be **wrong**. A figure with no possible falsifier is a characterization, not a measurement, and is labelled as one
- [ ] The artifact records who, **other than the assertor**, has checked it — over what scope, on what date
- [ ] **BLOCKING.** Where nobody independent has checked it, the artifact records **NO ASSURANCE**, in those words. Not "limited assurance", not "management-monitored", not "covered by process"
- [ ] The verdict is one of: RELIABLE FOR THE PURPOSE / RELIABLE WITH STATED LIMITS / NOT ESTABLISHED. **"Probably fine" does not appear**
- [ ] The NO ASSURANCE rule is marked `CONSTRUCTION`

## 5. The three states — the distinction the method turns on

**Described, operated and tested are three separate facts.** Merging them into one status word is
how a policy document gets presented to a board as a working control.

- [ ] **BLOCKING.** Every specific control carries **three separate marks**: DESCRIBED (a document says it exists), OPERATED (evidence it ran in the period), TESTED (someone independent checked it — named, with a date and a scope)
- [ ] **BLOCKING.** The three are **never merged** into a single status, in the artifact or in any summary of it. "In place", "effective" and "operating" do not appear as combined statuses
- [ ] A control DESCRIBED with no OPERATED evidence is stated, in those words, to be a control the board may not rely on
- [ ] A control OPERATED with no TESTED entry is marked as **self-asserted**
- [ ] The control environment is weighted first, and where it is weak the artifact states explicitly that the specific controls beneath it are **operating in spite of the environment** and are most likely to fail under exactly the pressure that makes them matter
- [ ] Controls dependent on a **single person** are identified, as are controls where the **preparer is also the reviewer**
- [ ] Deficiencies are rated by whether they could allow a material misstatement of something the board relies on — **not** by how hard they are to fix
- [ ] Every deficiency records where it was routed and when. A deficiency logged and not communicated has been documented, not addressed
- [ ] The three-state rule is marked `CONSTRUCTION`

## 6. Assurance mapping

- [ ] Reliances were built **backwards from board decisions**, not taken from a management-produced control inventory
- [ ] Each assurance entry records source, **scope (what exactly was covered)**, independence, date and result
- [ ] Each source is placed on the three-lines model, with external assurance recorded separately
- [ ] **BLOCKING.** **LINE COLLAPSE** — the same function both performing and assuring — is flagged explicitly wherever present, and is not netted into a coverage figure
- [ ] **Over-assurance** is surfaced as well as gaps
- [ ] **BLOCKING.** The **NO ASSURANCE list** appears explicitly, in those words, with the consequence of each uncovered reliance stated
- [ ] A period-on-period diff is present, and any **narrowing** of coverage is called out
- [ ] The **scope-exclusion question** was asked: what was proposed for the audit plan and removed, and by whom? Independence is lost at scoping more often than at the point of conclusion
- [ ] The **oldest open finding** is named, not the average age
- [ ] Findings closed on the owner's own confirmation, with no independent re-test, are marked `CLOSED — SELF-ASSERTED`

## 7. Fraud risk — framed structurally

- [ ] **BLOCKING.** The artifact states **at the start** that this is a **structural** question, not a question about anyone's honesty, and that it has an answer regardless of anyone's character. Framing it as a question about people is what makes it undiscussable
- [ ] The core question is answered: **which figure could a single person move, alone, without another party detecting it** — following the actual permissions, not the policy
- [ ] Where the three conditions commonly considered together in this discipline are used — pressure or incentive, opportunity, and rationalization — the artifact notes that **only opportunity is a structural variable the board can act on directly**, and attributes the triad to **standard practice in the discipline rather than to a single source**
- [ ] Incentive mapping is present: which figure determines someone's compensation, continued funding or personal standing. Every such figure is a candidate automatically
- [ ] Separation is checked: preparer versus reviewer; ability to initiate versus ability to approve; ability to alter the record after approval, and whether there is a log
- [ ] **Management override** is addressed explicitly as the standing exception in every control system, answered by independent evidence rather than by more controls
- [ ] The output is an **opportunity list with the detection gap for each**, and the specific evidence that would close it
- [ ] No individual is characterized. The artifact names structures, not suspects

## 8. Auditor independence and private session

- [ ] Structure is assessed first: **who appoints the auditor, who sets the fee, who sets the scope, who can dismiss them**. Where management effectively controls any of these, independence is described as structurally compromised regardless of intent
- [ ] The non-audit to audit fee ratio is shown over three periods and trending, framed **structurally** — does the fee relationship make a difficult audit conversation more expensive for the auditor to have? — not as value for money
- [ ] Non-audit services are enumerated, and any where the auditor would effectively be auditing its own work or making a management decision is identified
- [ ] Firm tenure and lead-partner tenure are recorded with the date of the last rotation
- [ ] Relationships are checked: former audit staff now in the organization, especially in finance or reporting roles; personal relationships; joint commercial interests
- [ ] Verdict is INDEPENDENT / INDEPENDENT WITH SPECIFIED THREATS AND SAFEGUARDS / NOT STRUCTURALLY INDEPENDENT, with the **deciding factor named**
- [ ] **BLOCKING.** The artifact states that whether a specific service is **permissible under applicable rules is a regulatory question for qualified advisers** — it assesses the structural position only
- [ ] Private sessions are confirmed as **standing** items. Where one had to be convened, the artifact notes that the act of convening is itself a signal that will suppress what the session was meant to surface
- [ ] The session record captures **what was said and, separately, what was noticeably not said**
- [ ] Any unresolved concern raised in session has an owner and a date recorded before the session ends

## 9. Professional limit — mandatory, no exceptions

- [ ] **BLOCKING.** The professional-limit block appears verbatim in the artifact
- [ ] **BLOCKING.** The artifact states that **this is not an audit and issues no audit opinion of any kind** — statutory, internal or otherwise — and that no contributing agent is a licensed auditor
- [ ] **BLOCKING.** The artifact does not attest to the effectiveness of internal control for any regulatory purpose
- [ ] Where the artifact marks a control TESTED, it states that it is recording that a **named party says they tested it** — not confirming that they did, and not adopting their conclusion
- [ ] **BLOCKING.** The artifact states it is **input for review by the audit committee, the directors and licensed professionals** — never the final assurance position
- [ ] **BLOCKING.** Accounting-policy determinations, statutory audit opinions, listing-rule questions, tax treatment, contract interpretation and the question of whether any statute applies are all REFERRED OUT — none answered, none approximated
- [ ] The human-review clause closes the artifact
- [ ] **Any allegation of fraud, misconduct or retaliation is surfaced in the open and routed to the whistleblowing channel immediately** — never summarized into a caveat or a footnote

## 10. Attribution

- [ ] **BLOCKING.** The artifact states that **audit committee practice has no single canonical author and no one foundational text**, and does not manufacture one
- [ ] Each provision used is attributed to its **named source**, or marked `DISCIPLINE` (common practice, no traceable origin, attributed to nobody), or `CONSTRUCTION` (this agent's test)
- [ ] **BLOCKING.** COSO *Internal Control — Integrated Framework* (originally 1992, updated 2013) is kept **distinct from** COSO *Enterprise Risk Management — Integrating with Strategy and Performance* (2017), which belongs to risk-oversight. The two are never merged or cited for each other's content
- [ ] Where Sarbanes-Oxley (2002) is referenced, the artifact states that it is **United States legislation applying to particular categories of issuer**, referenced for its structural principles, and **not asserted to bind this organization** — applicability is a legal question
- [ ] The three-lines model is attributed to the **Institute of Internal Auditors with the explicit caveat that its formulation has changed between versions**, and the reader is directed to the current source where precise wording matters
- [ ] Cadbury provisions used here are attributed to the Cadbury Report (December 1992) and its Code of Best Practice, with the note that fuller treatment belongs to governance-counsel
- [ ] **BLOCKING.** No section number, provision, title, year or author has been invented. Where exact text was uncertain, the artifact describes the principle and directs the reader to the source
- [ ] Where the discipline is applied to non-financial assertions, the **adaptation is declared in the body** — the transfer is by analogy and invokes no financial-reporting authority

## 11. Boundary

- [ ] Nothing here designs risk appetite — `risk-oversight`
- [ ] Nothing here rules on board composition or independence of non-executives as a governance structure — `governance-counsel`
- [ ] Nothing here assesses succession or leadership capability — `succession-lead`
- [ ] Nothing here is a code quality gate — `@qa` owns those; this artifact may rely on their output as an assertion, and treats it exactly as any other assertion
- [ ] Nothing drafts an epic (`@pm`), a story (`@sm`), an implementation (`@dev`) or a release (`@devops`)
