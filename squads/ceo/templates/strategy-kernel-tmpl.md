## 5. Source of power

> Which one the policy relies on. Name exactly one primary source; a policy relying on all
> of them relies on none.

- [ ] **Leverage** — anticipation + a pivot point + concentration on it. *Amplifying mechanism must be nameable.*
- [ ] **Proximate objective** — an objective close enough that the path is knowable now.
- [ ] **Chain-link repair** — one link caps the system; improving others yields nothing.
- [ ] **Design** — a configuration of resources and policies producing more than the parts.
- [ ] **Focus** — concentration of force where the effect is disproportionate.
- [ ] **Advantage** — relevant, paid for, and hard to copy.
- [ ] **Dynamics** — a structural shift underway, with a closing window.
- [ ] **Rival inertia** — a competitor structurally unable to respond quickly.

[SOURCE: Rumelt, sources of power]

**Selected:** [source]
**Amplifying mechanism:** [How a modest concentrated effort produces a disproportionate effect.]
**Conditions that must hold:** [What must remain true.]
**Observation that would show it has stopped holding:** [Named observation.]
