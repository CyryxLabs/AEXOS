# Regulated Referral Gate Checklist

**Checklist ID:** BA-CL-005
**Referenced by:** `admin-chief` (Steward)
**Applies to:** every incoming request to the business-admin squad, **before** triage; and to every
consolidated brief, arbitration record, sequence plan or referral log before it is captured.

> **SQUAD-WIDE PROFESSIONAL LIMIT.** This squad operates management frameworks beside four regulated
> professions. No agent is an accountant, tax adviser, auditor, lawyer, HR professional or compliance
> officer. This checklist exists because the most damaging error available here is a **competent
> answer to a question that should have left the organisation** — damaging precisely because the
> answer sounds useful.
>
> **Nothing in this squad is privileged.** Assume everything written is disclosable.
>
> This checklist authorises nothing. It only establishes whether a request may be worked at all.

## Section 1 — The gate. Runs before anything else.

Classify the request against every row. **A match means that part leaves the squad**: no specialist
is assigned to it, no partial view is offered, and the question is written for the professional
named.

### 1.1 Accountant

- [ ] Does it ask **how any item is recognised, measured, disclosed or classified**?
- [ ] Does it concern **bookkeeping, closing or statutory reporting**?
- [ ] Would the figure be **prepared for external reliance**?
- [ ] Is it a **valuation for a transaction**?

> Never answered by `finance-lead`, which reads and questions figures and states no treatment.
> **Bring them:** the underlying document or contract, the period, the reporting basis, and the
> specific question.

### 1.2 Tax adviser

- [ ] Any **tax treatment, planning question or filing position**?
- [ ] Anything destined for a **tax authority**?

> Never answered by any agent in this squad.
> **Bring them:** the transaction documents, the jurisdictions involved, the decision being made.

### 1.3 Auditor

- [ ] Any **assurance, audit, review or certification**?
- [ ] Any statement that **books or controls are correct**?

> Never answered by any agent in this squad. `finance-lead`'s readings are explicitly not assurance.
> **Bring them:** the statements, the period, the scope of assurance required.

### 1.4 Lawyer

- [ ] What a **clause means, permits or triggers**?
- [ ] **Enforceability, validity, compliance, lawfulness**?
- [ ] **Negotiating positions, drafting, redlining**?
- [ ] Any **dispute, claim, demand, threat**?
- [ ] Any **regulator or authority** contact?
- [ ] **Records retention or data protection**?
- [ ] **Corporate structure or delegations** — whether a delegation binds the organisation?

> Never answered by `legal-ops`, which runs the process around legal work and never the legal work.
> **Bring them:** the executed documents, a factual chronology, the specific question, the decision
> it supports, the deadline.

### 1.5 Qualified HR and employment counsel

- [ ] Any **individual employment matter** — grievance, discipline, dismissal, accommodation,
      investigation, improvement plan, or anything concerning a **named person**?
- [ ] **Lawfulness of any people practice**?
- [ ] **Consultation obligations, works councils, collective agreements**?
- [ ] **Employee-data processing**?

> Never answered by `people-lead`, which designs practices and handles no individual case.
> **Bring them:** the role definition, documented history with dates, the contract and any
> collective agreement, the jurisdiction.

### 1.6 Control owner

- [ ] Whether any **financial, legal or regulatory control may be removed, weakened or retimed**?
- [ ] Anything touching **segregation of duties**?

> Never answered by `process-lead`, which proposes with evidence and routes.
> **Bring them:** the control inventory entry — cost, catch rate, why it was introduced, and what
> now addresses that risk.

### 1.7 Gate verdict

- [ ] **Verdict recorded:** INSIDE / **OUTSIDE** / **SPLIT**
- [ ] If OUTSIDE or SPLIT: the professional is **named by type** and, where known, by person and firm
- [ ] The question is **written in the form the professional can answer directly**
- [ ] What to bring them is listed
- [ ] The referral is **recorded**, so the answer can later be matched to what was asked
- [ ] If SPLIT: the split is explicit — which part stays, which part leaves, and **where the seam
      is**, marked so nobody implements past it
- [ ] **No partial answer was given** on any OUTSIDE component — not "generally speaking", not "in
      most cases", not "not advice, but". Each of those is how the boundary is crossed in practice.
- [ ] **No OUTSIDE component was routed to a squad specialist** so it could be answered indirectly.
      Routing is not a laundering mechanism, and the intermediary makes it look sanctioned.
- [ ] Where classification was unclear, it was treated as **regulated**. Over-referring costs an
      email; under-referring costs a filing, a claim or a case.

## Section 2 — Privilege. Absolute.

- [ ] It has been stated that **nothing in this squad is privileged**, before anything sensitive was
      written.
- [ ] The matter has been assessed as contentious / potentially contentious / neither, and the
      assessment is recorded.
- [ ] If contentious or potentially contentious: **written documentation stopped**, counsel was
      routed, and the artefact records the freeze with its date.
- [ ] Nowhere does any artefact imply that squad workflow is protected. An unprivileged record of a
      contentious matter is disclosable and unhelpful.

---

## Section 3 — Routing quality (only reached if Section 1 permits work inside the squad)

- [ ] **3.1.** The request was **restated in the owning discipline's vocabulary** and the restatement
      was **confirmed with the requester** before proceeding. Silent reframing means the requester
      takes the answer as a response to their actual question.
- [ ] **3.2.** The reframing patterns were checked. In this domain the stated question is frequently
      not the owned one — a cash problem is often a contract-terms or process problem; a hiring-speed
      problem is often a decision-rights problem; "let us remove the approvals" is an analysis the
      squad can do and a decision it cannot make.
- [ ] **3.3.** **Exactly one owning specialist** was named. Broadcasting to several produces partial
      answers on differing unstated assumptions and no decision.
- [ ] **3.4.** The near-miss disciplines are named with the reason each was excluded.
- [ ] **3.5.** A short usable answer was given **only** where the question is genuinely a management
      question; where it is not, none was given and the reason was stated.
- [ ] **3.6.** The chief did **not** answer as a specialist. Producing a financial reading, a hiring
      design or a process redesign because the answer seemed obvious bypasses the method that makes
      it defensible and creates an artefact no specialist owns.
- [ ] **3.7.** A **written handoff brief** exists so the specialist starts with context instead of
      re-eliciting basics.
- [ ] **3.8.** Where several specialists are needed, a **sequence** was produced rather than a
      broadcast, ordered by dependency: obligations before cost; cost before pay design; contract
      terms before collection process; process map before any automation question.
- [ ] **3.9.** What gets **wasted by running them out of order** is stated — usually a named artefact
      that has to be rewritten.
- [ ] **3.10.** Steps blocked by an outstanding professional referral are marked. These are the real
      blockers and they consume external time.
- [ ] **3.11.** Requests that have left the administrative surface are routed to the correct core
      agent: `@pm` for epic and PRD, `@sm` for stories, `@po` for validation and backlog,
      `@architect` for system design, `@data-engineer` for schema and instrumentation, `@analyst` for
      deep research, `@dev` for implementation, `@qa` for testing, **`@devops` for git push, PRs, MCP
      and CI/CD — exclusive, no exceptions**.
- [ ] **3.12.** The routing decision was **written to the repository**, not left in a conversation.

---

## Section 4 — Artefact audit (consolidated briefs, arbitrations, referral logs)

- [ ] **4.1.** The squad-wide professional-limit notice is at the **top**.
- [ ] **4.2.** **Outstanding referrals and reviews appear before the findings**, not as a footnote. A
      brief that reads as concluded and carries its preconditions at the end will be implemented
      without them.
- [ ] **4.3.** Every statement traces to a specialist artefact, and every figure to the source that
      artefact named. **The brief generates nothing** (Constitution Article IV).
- [ ] **4.4.** No professional's advice is recorded in **summarised** form. It is referenced and
      attached — a summary loses the qualifications and becomes the version people quote.
- [ ] **4.5.** Every referral records: date, professional type, the **exact question sent**, what was
      provided with it, the decision it supports, and the deadline.
- [ ] **4.6.** Referrals outstanding past deadline are flagged, and it is stated whether one is
      currently the critical path.
- [ ] **4.7.** The coherence chain — commitment, cost, process, people, evidence — is walked, with
      each link's artefact and date recorded.
- [ ] **4.8.** Breaks are classified **independent or inherited**, and the repair order is
      **upstream first**. Inherited breaks are not repaired directly.
- [ ] **4.9.** Artefact dates are recorded. Staleness is frequently the cause of an apparent
      contradiction, and the repair then runs in the opposite direction from the obvious one.
- [ ] **4.10.** Contradictions are **surfaced plainly**, not smoothed by narration.
- [ ] **4.11.** No contradiction was resolved by **averaging**. Averaging manufactures an unevidenced
      claim from two evidenced ones.
- [ ] **4.12.** Where a disagreement turns on a regulated determination, **neither specialist
      decides** — the professional does, and that is recorded as the resolution.
- [ ] **4.13.** Where neither side has evidence, the output is a **deciding check to run**, not a
      decision.
- [ ] **4.14.** The artefact that must now be revised is named. A losing position left unrevised will
      be cited again.
- [ ] **4.15.** Recommendations and **what the squad does not know** are stated separately.
- [ ] **4.16.** No squad output crosses into epic framing, story drafting, implementation or release.
- [ ] **4.17.** Versioned file under `docs/` with date, named owner and version.

## Verdict

| Condition | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Any Section 1 row matched and the component was answered, softened, or routed inward | **VIOLATION.** Not fixable by adding a caveat. Remove the answer and issue the referral. |
| Any Section 1 row matched and was correctly referred | **REFERRED** — record it and proceed on the remainder only |
| Section 2 not satisfied on a contentious matter | **STOP.** Freeze documentation, route to counsel. |
| Sections 1 and 2 clean, Section 3 has gaps | **WORKABLE WITH FIXES** — apply them before handoff |
| Sections 1 and 2 clean, Section 3 clean | **CLEARED FOR ROUTING** |
| Section 4 audit with 4.1–4.6 unsatisfied | **NOT PUBLISHABLE** |

## The three failures this checklist exists to prevent

1. **Answering a regulated question in any framing.** There is no small version of practising a
   regulated profession, and hedged versions get relied on precisely because they sounded careful.
2. **Routing a regulated question inward** so a specialist answers it indirectly. The same violation
   with an intermediary, and worse, because the routing makes it look sanctioned.
3. **Skipping the gate under urgency.** Urgency is the specific condition under which boundaries get
   crossed. It is also usually the reason the outward referral needed to start sooner — if the
   question belongs to a lawyer or an accountant, the fastest path is finding that out now, not
   after an hour inside the squad producing something that cannot be used.
