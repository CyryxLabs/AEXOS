# Obligation Register — [AGREEMENT / PORTFOLIO] — v[N]

> **BOUNDARY NOTICE — READ BEFORE ANY ENTRY.** This register is an **administrative extraction**.
> It records **what an executed document states and where it states it** — a date, a duration, a
> notice period, a deliverable, a clause reference. It **never** records what a clause means, what
> it permits, what it triggers, or whether a term is favourable, standard, onerous or risky. That
> distinction is the entire difference between administration and advice.
>
> Legal operations is not the practice of law. Nobody producing this register is a lawyer, none
> holds a bar admission, and no legal advice is given here under any framing — not as an opinion,
> not as a view, not as "what usually happens", not as a first draft for counsel to correct.
>
> **NOTHING HERE IS PRIVILEGED.** Legal professional privilege attaches to lawyers and its
> operation depends on jurisdiction and circumstance — itself a counsel question. Assume every
> line of this document is disclosable. **If this matter is or could become contentious, stop
> populating this register and ask counsel how they want the matter documented from this point.**
>
> Organising reference: the legal operations discipline, most widely organised through the core
> competency model published by CLOC (Corporate Legal Operations Consortium), a **professional
> association framework rather than a single-author work**. It has been revised since first
> publication; read the current version from CLOC's own published materials before citing it
> externally rather than relying on any description of it here.

### Rules that govern every row

| Rule | Statement |
|---|---|
| Clause reference mandatory | No reference, no row. Not estimated, not inferred from a similar agreement. |
| Owner is a person | An obligation owned by "the company", "the team" or "operations" is owned by nobody and will surface late. |
| Lead time is mandatory | How far ahead the owner must be warned. Without it the register records the deadline and misses the decision. |
| Action date is the real date | Deadline minus lead time. Alerts fire on the action date, not the deadline. |
| No characterisation | Never "onerous", "standard", "favourable", "low risk", "market". Each is a legal assessment. |
| No summary of the agreement | A register is not a précis. A précis is an interpretation. |
| Auto-renewal flagged prominently | It is the most common source of unintended commitment. |

## 4. AMBIGUOUS list — routed to counsel, **not** entered as facts

An item belongs here when the date or the duty depends on **interpreting** the wording rather than
**reading** it. Ambiguity is the boundary marker. It is where extraction stops.

| # | Clause ref | What the document says | Why it cannot be extracted administratively | Question written for counsel | Decision it blocks | Deadline | Sent | Answered |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [§] | [quoted] | [e.g. "the notice period runs from an event whose occurrence is not determinable from the document"] | [one sentence counsel can answer directly] | [decision] | [date] | [date] | [date] |

**Never resolve an ambiguous item by choosing the reading that seems obvious, by adopting what
similar contracts usually say, or by asking another squad agent.** Routing a clause question inward
is the same violation with an intermediary attached, and the intermediary makes it look sanctioned.

**Ambiguous items do not appear in section 2 in any form**, including as a provisional row with a
note. A provisional row becomes a fact the moment someone reads the register in a hurry.

renew.** Cost input comes from `@business-admin:finance-lead`; anything about what the clause
permits comes from counsel.

## Template usage notes

- Sections 0, 4 and the boundary notice are not optional. A register without a routed AMBIGUOUS
  list is a register that quietly answered the hard questions.
- If a request arrives asking what a clause means — including "just summarise it", "roughly what
  does this commit us to", or "you read contracts all day" — the answer is a clean refusal plus an
  offer to assemble the counsel instruction pack. There is no partial legal answer available here,
  and a partial one is the failure mode of this role.
- The moment a matter looks contentious, this register stops being the right place to record
  anything. Raise the privilege warning first, then route.
