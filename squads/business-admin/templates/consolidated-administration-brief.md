## 5. Administrative coherence chain

The commitment recorded in the contract register, the cash consequence in the financial reading,
the process that executes it, the people who run that process, and the evidence it produced must
all describe the same reality. When they do not, **that is the finding**.

| Link | Owner | Artefact | What it says | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Commitment** | `legal-ops` | [#] | [statement] | OK / **BREAK** |
| **Cost** | `finance-lead` | [#] | [statement] | OK / BREAK |
| **Process** | `process-lead` | [#] | [statement] | OK / BREAK |
| **People** | `people-lead` | [#] | [statement] | OK / BREAK |
| **Evidence** | `admin-chief` | — | [statement] | OK / BREAK |

### Contradiction checks

| Check | Test | Result | Typical cause when it fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| Untracked commitment | Does every dated obligation in the register have a named owner and a calendar entry? | [pass/fail] | The lifecycle stops at signature; the post-signature stage was never defined |
| Terms versus cash | Do the payment terms in the register match the receivable days in the financial reading? | [pass/fail] | Terms granted commercially without a cash reading, or terms stated and not enforced by the collection process |
| Process without an owner | Does every process step have a named role, and does that role exist in the people design? | [pass/fail] | A redesign assigned work to a role nobody has been hired or trained for |
| Control counted twice | Is a control the financial reading relies on the same one a process redesign proposes to remove? | [pass/fail] | Two specialists working the same step from opposite ends without a shared inventory |
| Evidence gap | Could an outside reviewer verify each material claim from an artefact in the repository? | [pass/fail] | Decisions made in conversation and never captured, or captured without their sources |
| Referral not recorded | Does every regulated question sent outward appear in section 0 with the question asked? | [pass/fail] | Verbal referral; the answer then arrives without anyone knowing what was asked |

### Breaks, classified and ordered

| # | Break | Independent or **inherited**? | Upstream cause | Repair owner | Order |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [break] | independent | — | `@business-admin:[agent]` | 1 |
| 2 | [break] | inherited from #1 | [#1] | — | do not repair directly |

**A break in any link invalidates every link downstream of it, not only the adjacent one. Repair
upstream first.** Inherited breaks are not repaired directly; the upstream one is.
