## 2. Assessed attributes — at most five, each owned by one interviewer

that reads as signal. Each attribute is assessed by **one named interviewer**, so nobody assesses

## 3. Disqualifiers — listed separately from the scored attributes

Disqualifiers are not low scores. They are conditions under which the loop ends regardless of the
rest of the packet, and they must be written down because an unwritten disqualifier is applied
inconsistently.

|---|---|---|---|

**Every disqualifier requires explicit counsel review.** This is the item most likely to create
unlawful exclusion, and it is usually written last and reviewed least.

## 4. Evidence recording format — evidence before verdict

Every interviewer records, **in this order**:

1. **What the candidate said and did.** Specific, quoted or described. Not adjectives.
2. **The rubric level assigned**, against the anchor in section 2.
3. **The interviewer's reasoning** connecting 1 to 2.

| Rule | Statement |
|---|---|
| Rubric written before candidates | The rubric in section 2 exists before the first candidate is seen. A rubric written after is a rationalisation. |
| Independent submission | No interviewer discusses a candidate with another interviewer before submitting. Discussion before submission converts several independent signals into one shared impression. |
| No verdict-only packets | "Strong communicator" is worthless. A packet must be reviewable by someone who was not in the room. |
| Same questions, same order | Every candidate for this role gets the same fixed questions per attribute, in the same order. |

## 7. Decision rights — separated from the person with the vacancy

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Who decides | [named deciding body or independent reviewer — **not** the hiring manager alone] |
| What they decide on | The packet: this bar, recorded evidence per attribute, the work sample and its score, and the recommendation with reasoning |
| Decision rule, written in advance | [what evidence pattern is a hire; what is a no. Ambiguity here is where urgency enters.] |
| Escalation for genuine disagreement | [a decision route — not a re-run of the loop until the desired answer appears] |
| Exception path | [written down, reviewed, visible. Lowering the bar must be a visible act, not a quiet one.] |
| Retrospective loop | Revisit hires against this bar after [period] to see whether the bar predicted anything |

**Why the separation exists.** The person with the open role carries the urgency. Separating the
decision from that person is what protects the bar when the role has been open too long, which is
exactly when it drifts.

## 8. Transfer caveats — stated because the source's context is not this organisation

The framework's practices were developed inside one very large, unusually selective, unusually
well-funded organisation. Some transfer intact; some do not.

| Element | Transfers? | Adaptation for this organisation |
|---|---|---|
| Structure: fixed questions, pre-written rubric, anchored levels | **Fully** — the mechanism is the transferable part | none needed |
| Independent submission before discussion | **Fully** | none needed |
| Decision separated from the hiring manager | **Mechanism yes, implementation no** | A hiring committee becomes a single independent reviewer |
| Multi-round loops | **No** — committee overhead assumes volume this organisation does not have | Two rounds with defined purposes |
| Work samples | **Fully** | Keep the time bound short; compensate anything substantial |
| Large-scale calibration machinery | **No** | Lighter calibrated judgement, same evidence discipline |

**On the research the source cites.** The framework reports published research finding that
structured methods and work samples substantially outperform unstructured interviews at predicting
performance. This document reproduces **the direction of that finding only**. It does not reproduce
specific validity coefficients, because a misquoted statistic is worse than a described one. If a
number is needed for an external argument, read the underlying research directly rather than
quoting this document.

**Do not import a practice because a well-known company uses it.** Import it because the mechanism
behind it applies here. The mechanism is the transferable part.

## Template usage notes

  separately-listed disqualifiers, or without its transfer caveats is not this artefact.
- If at any point the work requires discussing a named person, their performance, their conduct,
  their pay or their employment status — **stop**. That is qualified HR and employment counsel, in
  full, immediately, and it does not belong in this document at any stage of drafting.
- Constitution Article IV — No Invention: every claim about what works traces to the cited
  framework, to an internal measurement, or to a stated assumption. Unsupported people-practice
