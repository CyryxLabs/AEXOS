## Step 1 — The Warmer

**Purpose:** demonstrate context by naming the problems commonly seen in accounts like theirs, *before* asking any questions.

> **Say:** "{{Before you tell me about X, let me tell you what we usually see in {{their type of team}}, and you can tell me how much of it is wrong. Three things come up almost every time: {{a}}, {{b}}, {{c}}.}}"

**Success signal:** they correct one of the three. **The correction is the most useful data in the meeting** — record it verbatim.
**If they agree with all three:** the warmer was too generic. Note it; the reframe will land weakly.

## Step 2 — The Reframe

**Purpose:** introduce the surprising element — the real problem is not the one they named.

> **Say:** "{{the reframe, delivered as an observation with evidence}}"

**Delivered as:** an observation, with the evidence visible. **Not** as a challenge to their competence.
**The why-it-was-invisible line (mandatory):** "{{...}}"
**Evidence shown at this moment:** {{the distribution, the chart, the regulatory figure — name it}}

**Success signal:** engagement with the substance, **including disagreement**. Polite agreement is a weak signal.
**If they contest the data:** good. The reframe is landing. Ask what they see that contradicts it — the answer is one of: their situation differs and the insight needs a segment boundary (we just learned it), our evidence is weak on a specific point (strengthen that point), or they are testing whether we hold a position under mild pressure (folding immediately costs more than the disagreement did). Do not soften before finding out which.

## Step 3 — Rational Drowning

**Purpose:** quantify the consequences rigorously, in **their** units, using **their** figures wherever possible.

| Input | Value | Whose number |
|---|---|---|
| {{their systems count / volume / duration}} | | theirs |
| {{our distribution or benchmark}} | | ours — labelled |
| {{the salary, cost or rate}} | | **ask them to supply it** |

> **Ask:** "{{the question that makes them supply or correct a number}}"

**Success signal:** the buyer supplies or corrects a number, making the case partly theirs.
**This step should be uncomfortable. That is the function of the step** — and the discomfort comes from evidence about their own operation, never from anything we invented.

## Step 4 — Emotional Impact

**Purpose:** connect the quantified consequence to *their* situation so it stops being an industry statistic.

> **Say:** "{{Reference something they described, in their words, with its date. e.g. 'You mentioned the board asked about the slip last quarter. If X lands in Q4 and the problem is still there in Q1, what does that conversation look like?'}}"

**Source of the situation referenced:** {{their words, {{date}} — never a manufactured scenario}}
**Success signal:** they describe a specific instance unprompted.

## Tailoring — one row per stakeholder

**The reframe stays constant. Only the concrete consequence changes.** Telling each stakeholder a different version of the problem is discovered the moment the buying group compares notes.

| Stakeholder | What they are measured on | What this change costs them personally | What they risk if it fails | The one sentence they would repeat to a colleague |
|---|---|---|---|---|

**Who loses from this change:** {{name them}}
**Their likely objection:** {{...}}

> That person is not a blocker to be routed around. Their objection is usually the most accurate information available in the account. Engage early.

## Control moves

| Where the conversation drifts | The return move (exact wording) |
|---|---|
| Feature comparison | "{{a question that puts them back on their own stated goal}}" |
| Indefinite information gathering | "{{...}}" |
| Deferral to an absent decider | "{{...}} — and route the access question to `@sales:qualification-lead`}}" |
| Deflection | "{{name what we are observing and ask them to confirm or correct it}}" |

**Control means directing the conversation toward the decision that has to be made. It never means directing the person toward our conclusion.** If the buyer feels cornered rather than informed, the tension has stopped being constructive and the method has been misapplied.

## Tension screen — run before this plan is used

Every planned move is tested. Any failure blocks the move; the compliant alternative replaces it.

| Move | Is the pressure a real constraint on our side or theirs? | Can every stated consequence be substantiated? | Does it imply scarcity, deadline or risk that does not exist? | Reversal test | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | | PASS / **BLOCKED** |

**Blocked moves and their honest replacements:**

| Blocked | Why | Compliant alternative (exact wording) |
|---|---|---|

**Construction rule:** state our real constraint, attribute it to us, and ask for theirs.
