# Negotiation Integrity Checklist

**Checklist ID:** SALES-CL-005
**Referenced by:** negotiation-lead (`*negotiation-prep`, `*integrity-screen`, `*concession-plan`, `*procurement-play`, `*post-mortem`)
**Purpose:** Screen a negotiation plan, or a single proposed move, before it reaches the counterparty. Every **BLOCK** item is a hard stop. A blocked move is removed and replaced with the compliant alternative — the version that states our real constraint and asks for theirs.

[[LLM: EXECUTION INSTRUCTIONS — NEGOTIATION INTEGRITY

1. Run BEFORE the call. A negotiation prepared in advance is a plan; a negotiation improvised is a
   series of concessions, and a screen applied afterwards is a post-mortem.
2. Items marked (BLOCK) stop the move outright. There is no "mostly compliant".
3. Every BLOCK produces a written compliant alternative in exact wording. If no legitimate goal
   survives the rewrite, state plainly that the legitimate goal does not exist.
4. Record the verdict against the deal. If a human proceeds against the screen, log it as decided
   against advice with the decider named.
5. Effectiveness is never one of the tests. "It works" does not clear an item.

ATTRIBUTION: framework is Chris Voss, Never Split the Difference (2016), written with Tahl Raz.
The book's citation of the 7-38-55 ratio (attributed to research by Albert Mehrabian) is contested
in the wider literature; nothing in this checklist depends on it and it is not repeated as settled.]]

## 0. Gate — is this a negotiation at all?

- [ ] **(BLOCK)** Qualification confirmed with `@sales:qualification-lead` before preparation began
- [ ] The economic buyer is known and our access to them is honestly stated
- [ ] The decision process is mapped and dated

> An unqualified deal is not a negotiation. It is a discount conversation with someone who cannot sign, and conceding in it teaches the pipeline that pressure works.

## 1. Urgency and deadlines

- [ ] **(BLOCK)** No deadline is stated that does not exist
- [ ] **(BLOCK)** No price expiry, reversion or "offer ends" is claimed unless it is an actual documented rule applied to any account
- [ ] Every deadline is attributed to whoever owns it. **Our quarter boundary is stated as ours** and never presented as the buyer's constraint or a market rule
- [ ] For each stated deadline, the answer to *"what actually happens the day after?"* is recorded. If it is nothing specific, it is a lever and is read as one — including when it is ours

> **Compliant construction:** "The structure I quoted is approved through the end of this cycle; after that I have to take it back through approval and I cannot promise the same terms come out the other side. That is my constraint, not a deadline for you. What would have to happen on your end for a decision to be possible before then?"

## 2. Scarcity

- [ ] **(BLOCK)** No implementation slot, capacity limit or allocation is claimed that does not exist and is not tracked somewhere checkable
- [ ] **(BLOCK)** No "another client is waiting" without a specific, real, named commitment
- [ ] Any genuine constraint could be evidenced if the counterparty asked to see it

> The practical cost, separate from the ethics: buyers talk to each other, procurement teams keep notes, and an invented slot that never materializes is remembered at renewal — the exact moment we have least leverage and most need to be believed.

## 3. Alternatives and bluffs

- [ ] **(BLOCK)** No competing offer, other buyer or alternative bidder is implied unless it exists
- [ ] **(BLOCK)** No claim about competitor behaviour, pricing or capability that we cannot source outside our own marketing
- [ ] Our leverage is represented from our **real** alternatives — pipeline coverage, whether we need this deal this quarter, what the capacity would otherwise do
- [ ] Their alternatives in the plan are marked VERIFIED or INFERRED, and **no concession is designed against an INFERRED alternative**

> A bluffed alternative is the cheapest claim in a negotiation to test: the counterparty simply slows down and waits. When it does not materialize, we have lost the claim and the credibility of everything else.

## 4. Walk-away

- [ ] **(BLOCK)** No walk-away is announced that we would not take
- [ ] The walk-away was written **before** the conversation, and its date is recorded
- [ ] It was not revised mid-call under pressure *(if it was, it was a preference — record the lesson)*
- [ ] The person with authority to change it is named, and it is not the rep under pressure
- [ ] If terms genuinely fall below the line, the plan says so plainly and without drama

> A calm, real no moves more than a loud, false one. A stated walk-away we do not mean is one of the few claims in a negotiation that gets tested directly, and when it is called we either walk unwillingly or reveal that our limits are theatre. Every limit after that is discounted, including the real ones.

## 5. Concessions

- [ ] **(BLOCK)** No concession is offered without a **named** non-price item it buys. "Goodwill" and "momentum" are not items
- [ ] Each step is materially smaller than the last
- [ ] The final number is non-round
- [ ] One reserve non-monetary item is held for close
- [ ] **(BLOCK)** Nothing outside the approved commercial structure is planned around. Anything outside it is marked ESCALATE and routed to `@products:pricing-strategist`
- [ ] The plan does not split the difference to end the discomfort
- [ ] If the counterparty has conceded nothing across two rounds, the plan stops conceding and asks a calibrated question about their process

## 6. Capability claims and disclosure

- [ ] **(BLOCK)** Every capability described exists **today**; roadmap is disclosed as roadmap with no implied date we have not committed to internally
- [ ] **(BLOCK)** Known functional limitations relevant to the buyer's criteria are disclosed
- [ ] **(BLOCK)** Known integration gaps are disclosed
- [ ] **(BLOCK)** Total cost is disclosed: implementation, onboarding, professional services, required third-party licences
- [ ] **(BLOCK)** Renewal mechanics are disclosed — uplift, auto-renewal window, repricing trigger
- [ ] Every reference, logo, case study or metric used is real and attributable

> Material omission converts a signature into churn and destroys the reference. It is not a technique.

## 7. Tactical empathy — used, not inverted

- [ ] **(BLOCK)** No label, mirror or question uses an understanding of the counterparty's fear to **press on it** rather than to serve their interest *(empathy inversion)*
- [ ] Labels are built to invite a **correction or an elaboration** — a label that invites a yes or no has failed and is rebuilt
- [ ] Calibrated questions open with **how** or **what**, not **why**
- [ ] No question is an argument with a question mark attached. If it cannot be answered honestly without conceding, it is a trap and is removed
- [ ] The accusation audit names the **largest genuine** objection, not a smaller decoy used as distraction

> The method is demonstrating accurate understanding so the counterparty will tell us the rest. Corrections are how we find out we were wrong, which is the whole value of the technique. Identifying a fear in order to lean on it is its exact inversion.

## 8. Pressure on the person

- [ ] No move implies personal or professional consequence for the individual if they do not proceed
- [ ] No move asks a champion or sponsor to assert something we have not verified
- [ ] Procurement is not gone around. The sponsor is re-engaged transparently, with procurement in the room
- [ ] Nothing in the plan treats a mandate ("I have a savings target") as unreasonableness to be argued down rather than a metric to be served where we can

## 9. The two whole-plan tests

- [ ] **(BLOCK) Reversal test.** If the counterparty learned exactly how every move was constructed, would they still consider the dealing fair?
- [ ] **(BLOCK) Durability test.** Could the counterparty defend the resulting agreement to their own organization if they knew everything we know?

> An agreement they could not defend is not a win. It is a renegotiation scheduled for renewal, usually with someone new in the seat.

## 10. Boundary

- [ ] Nothing here sets or revises price level, packaging, value metric or discount policy (`@products:pricing-strategist`)
- [ ] Nothing here defines a market category or competitive frame (`@products:positioning-lead`)
- [ ] No qualification verdict is issued here (`@sales:qualification-lead`)
- [ ] Repeated patterns — late procurement, end-of-period discounting, unqualified deals reaching negotiation — are routed to `@sales:pipeline-ops` as funnel findings
- [ ] Anything committed to the buyer that requires delivery is routed to `@pm` for epic framing; nothing is implemented, tested or released here
